## Cross-cutting findings
1. Authority ≠ safety (designs 2, 5, 6); per-command safety ≠ recoverability (3); the frozen common layer is a reactive controller that cannot protect states it is handed after a heading perturbation.
2. Designs 2, 3, 5 and 6 fail at the same boundary even though they differ in action space, state and strength: the closed loop of *any* heading perturbation + frozen reactive planner has a non-zero collision tail in clutter, and C0 (no obstacles) is collision-free in all of them.
3. Evasion is a trade, not a free gain: at the smallest strength with real authority (0.50) net captures are +2/2,000 while exhaustion rises 0 → 36.
4. Canonical A0 is collision-free by construction, so the strict gate (zero gained collisions) is demanding but well defined; it was not relaxed after seeing results (0.50 is the nearest miss: 4 gained collisions, all in C2/C3).
